## Cross-language patterns

1. **"Free" is the dominant commercial modifier in FR, ES, PT — and the entry-tier expectation in EN.** In EN listicles every major vendor's entry price prints as $0; Square built its whole EN/FR/ES presence on "free POS". A paid-only message fights the SERP in all four languages.
2. **Each language has a fiscal-compliance keyword that rides with "free":** FR = NF525 / loi anti-fraude; ES-MX = CFDI 4.0 / SAT facturación; PT-BR = NFC-e / NF-e / SEFAZ. These are not features, they are search terms buyers literally type.
3. **Offline is a first-class keyword outside EN:** "100 % hors ligne" (FR), "fuera de línea / sin conexión" (ES), "funcionar sem conexão / offline" (PT). It appears in titles, not feature lists.
4. **Category vocabulary differs per language — do not translate literally:**
   - EN: "POS system / POS software" (never "shop management software" — that SERP is auto-repair)
   - FR: "logiciel de caisse" + "logiciel point de vente / PDV"
   - ES: "software/sistema punto de venta" + "TPV" (Spain-ambiguous); never bare "programa"
   - PT: "sistema de gestão / ERP para lojas" with "PDV / frente de caixa" as the module term
5. **SERP ownership:** EN "best" queries = affiliate listicles (FitSmallBusiness, TechnologyAdvice, TechRepublic). FR "gestion magasin" = B2B marketplaces (HelloPro, Techni-Contact). ES "gratis" = Google Play + YouTube. PT "grátis" = vendor fiscal pages + one listicle + GitHub. GitHub/open-source ranks page-1 in every language — the organic bar for informational queries is low outside EN listicles.
6. **The universal incumbent set:** Square, Shopify POS, Lightspeed, Loyverse (dominant in ES/PT free-mobile), Odoo POS (the open-source baseline named in ES/PT), Zettle/SumUp (FR/EU).
