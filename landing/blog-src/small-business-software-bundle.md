The average small retailer now juggles an absurd number of software subscriptions: a POS system, an appointment book, an email marketing tool, a website builder, an inventory spreadsheet (or a separate inventory app), and maybe a loyalty program. Each one is $20–$80/month. Together, they're a second rent payment.

There's a better approach: one platform that does it all.

## What "Bundle" Should Mean

A real software bundle for small business isn't just a discounted package of separate tools. It should be:

- **One login.** Staff learn one system, not five.
- **Shared data.** A customer who books an appointment is the same customer who buys in-store. The system should know that.
- **One price.** Not five subscriptions with five renewal dates and five support contacts.
- **Consistent interface.** Learn it once, use it everywhere.

## What to Look for in an All-in-One Platform

**Point of sale.** The core till: fast checkout, discounts, returns, multiple payment types.

**Inventory.** Product catalog, stock tracking, low-stock alerts. This is the backbone — without it, you're guessing.

**Appointments.** For service businesses (salons, repair shops, clinics), booking with reminders reduces no-shows dramatically.

**Staff tools.** Time-clock, scheduling, individual logins.

**Online ordering.** Customers order from your website; orders land in your system. No commission fees to a delivery platform.

**Storefront page.** A simple public web page for your shop that stays in sync with your inventory.

**Gift cards and loyalty.** Keep customers coming back.

**Reporting.** Sales, products, staff, taxes — the numbers you need, in plain language.

**Backups.** Automatic, with the ability to download your full data anytime.

## The Hidden Cost of "Free" Tools

It's tempting to patch together free tools — a free POS tier, a free scheduling app, a free website builder. But free tools have costs too:

- **Transaction fees.** "Free" POS plans typically charge the highest transaction rates. On real revenue, this dwarfs any subscription savings.
- **Feature limits.** Free tiers cap your products, staff accounts, or transactions — and the upgrade that removes the cap is rarely cheap.
- **Your time.** Managing six disconnected free tools, each with its own quirks and limitations, costs hours every month. Your time has value.
- **Data fragmentation.** Customer info scattered across five systems means no single view of your business.

A single **$350/year** platform that's actually complete is cheaper than a stack of "free" tools once you count transaction fees and your time.
