# How Do I Switch POS Systems?

**Switching POS systems takes 4 steps: export your data from the old system, set up products in the new one, run both in parallel for 1–2 weeks, then cut over fully.** Most shops complete the switch in 2–4 weeks. The biggest mistakes are rushing the cutover and not exporting historical data before canceling the old subscription.

## Step 1: Export Everything From Your Current System

Before you touch anything else, download your data:

- **Product catalog** — names, SKUs, prices, categories, stock counts (usually a CSV export)
- **Customer list** — names, contact info, purchase history if available
- **Sales history** — at least 12 months for tax records and trend analysis
- **Gift card balances** — outstanding liabilities you owe customers
- **Staff records** — if your old system tracked time or commissions

Do this **before** canceling anything. Some providers restrict exports once you downgrade or close your account.

## Step 2: Set Up the New System

- **Import your product catalog** — most modern POS systems (including Vendra) accept CSV imports; map your columns once and the products load in bulk
- **Recreate your tax settings** — sales tax rates, exemptions, and receipt requirements vary by location; verify these match your jurisdiction
- **Set up staff accounts** — create logins with appropriate permissions for each team member
- **Connect your card reader** — pair the hardware and run a $1 test transaction (then refund it)
- **Configure receipts** — business name, return policy, and any legally required information

## Step 3: Run in Parallel

Don't switch overnight. For 1–2 weeks:

- Process real sales on the **new** system
- Keep the old system accessible for lookups and as a fallback
- Have staff practice on the new till during quiet hours
- Compare end-of-day totals between systems to catch setup errors

This parallel period catches 90% of migration problems — wrong tax rates, missing products, misconfigured printers — before they affect real business.

## Step 4: Cut Over and Cancel

Once the new system is running smoothly:

- Process all new sales on the new system only
- Keep the old system's data exported and archived (tax authorities may ask for records years later)
- Cancel the old subscription — but check for contract terms, early termination fees, or required notice periods first

## Common Switching Pitfalls

- **Forgetting gift card liabilities** — outstanding gift cards are debts you owe; make sure balances transfer
- **Losing sales history** — export before canceling; you can't get it back after
- **Contract auto-renewal** — some providers require 30–90 days' notice; check before your renewal date
- **Staff resistance** — involve your team early, train during paid hours, and acknowledge the learning curve
