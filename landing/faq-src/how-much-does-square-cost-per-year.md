# How Much Does Square Cost Per Year?

**A small shop doing $200,000/year in card sales pays roughly $5,950–$6,338/year for Square**, depending on the plan. That includes monthly subscription fees plus Square's per-transaction fees of 2.6% + $0.15 (Free plan), 2.5% + $0.15 (Plus at $49/month), or 2.4% + $0.15 (Premium at $149/month).

## Breaking Down Square's Real Annual Cost

Square's headline pricing looks simple, but the true cost has two parts:

**Part 1: Monthly subscription.**
- Free: $0/month — but highest transaction rates
- Plus: $49/month ($588/year)
- Premium: $149/month ($1,788/year)

**Part 2: Transaction fees on every sale.** This is where the real money goes. On $200,000 in annual card revenue with a $40 average ticket (5,000 transactions/year):
- Free plan: $200,000 × 2.6% + (5,000 × $0.15) = **$5,950/year**
- Plus plan: $588 + ($200,000 × 2.5%) + (5,000 × $0.15) = **$6,338/year**
- Premium plan: $1,788 + ($200,000 × 2.4%) + (5,000 × $0.15) = **$7,338/year**

**Part 3: Hardware (not included).** Card reader ($59), Square Stand ($149), Terminal ($299), Register ($799+). These are one-time costs but add to year one.

## Why the "Free" Plan Isn't Free

The $0/month Free plan sounds appealing, but it charges the highest transaction rate. For any shop doing meaningful volume, the transaction fees dwarf the subscription savings. There's no way to negotiate lower rates unless you qualify for the custom Pro tier ($250k+/year volume).

## Square's Pricing History: What Changed

In October 2025, Square overhauled its pricing, replacing the old industry-specific plans (Retail, Restaurants, Appointments) with three unified tiers: Free, Plus, and Premium. The restructuring simplified the plan lineup but kept the same fundamental model — monthly subscriptions stacked on top of per-transaction fees.

For existing Square users, the change meant re-evaluating which tier matched their old plan's features. For new shoppers comparing options, the key takeaway hasn't changed: the transaction fees are where the real money goes, regardless of which tier you pick.
