# Do POS Systems Charge Transaction Fees?

**Most POS systems charge transaction fees of 2.4–2.9% plus $0.10–$0.30 per sale, on top of their monthly subscription.** This is separate from what your payment processor charges. Some providers, like Vendra, charge $0 in transaction fees — you pay only the $350/year flat rate plus whatever your card processor charges at its standard rate.

## Two Different Kinds of Transaction Fees

The confusion comes from two layers that get blended together:

**1. Processor fees (unavoidable).** The company that moves the money — Stripe, Square Payments, etc. — charges roughly 2.6–2.9% + $0.15–$0.30 per transaction. Someone has to process the payment, and this is their cut. You pay this no matter which POS you use.

**2. POS platform fees (avoidable).** Many POS providers add their own per-transaction charge on top. Sometimes it's baked into a higher processing rate; sometimes it's a separate fee. Either way, it's revenue for the software company — not a cost of moving money.

## What the Big Providers Charge

- **Square:** 2.6% + $0.15 per in-person transaction (Free plan). Paid plans lower the rate slightly but add monthly fees — and still charge per transaction.
- **Lightspeed:** ~2.6% + $0.10 through their integrated processing, plus monthly subscription.
- **Shopify POS:** 2.4–2.7% + $0.10 depending on plan, plus monthly fees. Using a third-party processor instead of Shopify Payments adds a 2% penalty fee.
- **Toast:** 2.49–3.09% + $0.15, and Toast Payments is mandatory — you can't shop for a better processor rate.
- **Vendra:** $0. You bring your own processor (e.g., Stripe) and pay their standard rate. Vendra adds nothing per transaction.

## Why Do POS Companies Charge Per Transaction?

Because it's enormously profitable. A flat $350/year from 1,000 shops is $350,000 in revenue. But 2.6% of those shops' combined sales could be millions. Transaction fees turn the POS provider into a silent partner in your business — they earn more every time you succeed, without doing additional work.

## How to Avoid Transaction Fees

1. **Choose a POS with $0 platform transaction fees** — read the pricing page carefully; "no monthly fee" doesn't mean "no transaction fee"
2. **Use your own processor** — avoid providers that lock you into their processing
3. **Read the fine print on "free" plans** — they almost always have the highest transaction rates

## The Bottom Line

Transaction fees are the most expensive part of most POS systems — often 10–20× the subscription cost. Eliminating the platform's cut is the single biggest way to reduce your POS bill.
