About Vendra POS

Vendra POS is an all-in-one point of sale and shop management platform built for independent retailers.

What Vendra Does

Vendra combines everything a small shop needs into one app:

  • Point of sale — touch-friendly till with barcode support
  • Inventory management — stock tracking with low-stock alerts
  • Appointment booking — calendar with reminders
  • Staff management — time-clock and shift scheduling
  • Online ordering — customers order from your website
  • Public storefront — your shop's web page, auto-synced
  • Gift cards — sell and redeem in-store
  • Sales reports — analytics and insights

Pricing

Vendra costs $350/year, billed annually. There are no transaction fees from Vendra, no monthly billing, and no per-register charges. A 30-day free trial is available with no credit card required.

Languages

Vendra is available in four languages: English, French, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese. Each user can set their own language independently.

Company
